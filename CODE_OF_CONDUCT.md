# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. Be kind, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **fazalabbas2002@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly break it.
